Verve | Packaging & Identity

ideation - visaul identity - illustrator - graphic design

Description: Verev Vegan Broth is a conceptual brand and product developed as a passion project to showcase skills in branding, strategy, and graphic design. The project aimed to create a unique identity for a vegan broth tailored for individuals seeking a healthy, plant-based alternative to traditional bone broth. The resulting visual identity includes branding elements, packaging design, and a web shop, all crafted with attention to detail and a focus on conveying the brand's values and mission.
